# 09 · Location pages: "[service] agency in [city]"

Added 2026-10-09 after the owner pointed to **luminacommerce.com**, a remote-working agency ranking for "best Shopify agency in Dubai". This revises the "no city pages" stance in the first version of `02` §3.

## 1. What Lumina actually does (researched)

| Signal | What they have |
|---|---|
| **Location service pages** | `/pages/shopify-agency-dubai` with title "Best Shopify Agency in Dubai & UAE \| Shopify Agency \| LUMINA", plus `/best-seo-agency-in-dubai`, `/best-ppc-agency-in-dubai`, `/digital-marketing-agency-in-dubai`, `/performance-marketing-agency-dubai`. One page per service, all for the same market. |
| **Page content** | ~1,200–1,400 words. "Dubai"/"UAE" ~10 times (title, hero sub, FAQ "Do you work outside Dubai and the UAE?"). The rest is generic: no AED pricing, VAT, Tabby/Tamara, Arabic or local shipping. Mostly the city name inserted into a good service page. |
| **Authority (the real reason it ranks)** | **Shopify Partner directory: primary location Dubai, 5.0 from 57 reviews**, partner since 2019, Select tier. Clutch awards "Top Google Ads Company UAE 2025". DesignRush and Sortlist profiles. Real UAE clients (Christian Louboutin UAE, Nalbandian Carpets). Listed in "top Shopify agencies in Dubai" roundups. |
| **Location identity** | Their home base is Dubai (+971 phone, "based in Dubai" in the FAQ, directory location Dubai). They work remotely, but they're **anchored to one city** and rank in that city. |

**Lessons:**
1. I was too strict in v1. Google's **organic** results (not the Maps pack) for "best [service] agency in [city]" do include agencies with no office in that city, as long as the page is strong and the domain has authority. These queries are mostly answered by listicles, Clutch/DesignRush city rankings and agency service pages, not by Maps.
2. Their content is thin and still ranks because the **authority stack** (57 directory reviews, Clutch awards, real local clients) carries it. Copying their page style without their authority won't work in US cities, where competition is far heavier than in Dubai.
3. Their location is real. For us, **US cities are markets we serve, not places we're based**, so our pages must be more useful than theirs and honest about being remote.

## 2. Revised approach for the US

Three layers:

**Layer 1 · Country (biggest, do first).** Our home market is "the USA", the way Lumina's is "Dubai & UAE". Service pages carry the country in the title and copy:
- `Shopify Agency for US Brands · Stores from $4,900 · [Brand]`
- `App Development Company for US Small Businesses · [Brand]`
- Target: "shopify agency usa", "best app development company usa", "software development company usa", "white label web development usa". These are national queries with no map pack.

**Layer 2 · Metro service pages (new, controlled).** `[service] in [city]` pages for the largest US metros, **only** for services whose city searches show organic agency results (check each SERP first: if the top is 3 map pins + "near me" intent, skip that service in that city).

**Layer 3 · State pages (later, only if data supports).** E.g. "shopify developer texas". Usually lower volume; build only where Keyword Planner shows demand.

## 3. Which services get city pages

| Service | City pages? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify development | ✅ Yes | "shopify agency [city]" / "shopify developer [city]" has organic agency results and listicles; buyers are fine with remote |
| Mobile app development | ✅ Yes | "app developers [city]" / "app development company [city]" is big in every metro; Clutch city lists dominate, agency pages rank too |
| Custom software / web apps | ✅ Yes | "software development company [city]", "custom software [city]" |
| Web design (WordPress) | ⚠️ Selected cities | "web design [city]" is heavily local + map-pack; try 3 cities first, measure |
| AI / automation | ⚠️ Later | "ai automation agency [city]" is emerging; test in 2–3 tech metros |
| SEO | ❌ No | "seo agency [city]" is the most competitive local query in the US; Maps-dominated |
| Vibe rescue, payments, landing pages | ❌ No | Nobody searches these by city; national pages win |
| White-label | ❌ No | Agencies don't search by city |

## 4. Which cities, in which order

Start where we already have a story (concept brands become real local case studies as clients come in), then the biggest small-business markets.

| Wave | Cities | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (6) | **New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Dallas, Miami** | Largest small-business and ecommerce markets; Chicago (FreshFold), Dallas (Volt & Wire), Miami (FixPoint) already in our stories |
| 2 (6) | Austin, Phoenix, Denver, Atlanta, San Francisco Bay Area, Seattle | Austin (Maison Rose), Phoenix (Apex Auto), Denver (Peak Plumbing) in our stories; strong startup/SaaS demand |
| 3 (6+) | Boston, San Diego, Philadelphia, Orlando/Tampa, Charlotte, Nashville, Las Vegas | Growth metros; build when waves 1–2 show traction |

Wave 1 = 6 city hubs + 6×3 service pages = **24 pages**. Don't build wave 2 until wave 1 pages are indexed and at least half are on page 1–3 (Search Console).

## 6. How each city page beats Lumina's (and avoids the doorway-page trap)

Google's spam policy targets **sets of near-identical pages that differ only by place name**. Lumina gets away with thin pages in one city; with 24+ US pages we wouldn't. Each page needs **at least half its content written for that city**:

| Block | Example (Houston · Shopify) |
|---|---|
| Kicker H1 | "Shopify agency in Houston" + a city-specific headline ("Houston brands sell to all 50 states. Your store should handle it.") |
| Plain-English block | Who we work with in Houston, prices, timeline, "we work remotely on Central Time" |
| **Local market** | The Houston businesses we're built for (restaurants, energy-services suppliers, beauty brands, auto shops), with 2–3 real local numbers and their source |
| **Local rules that change the build** | Texas sales tax setup in Shopify, ship-from-Texas rates, Spanish storefront option (Houston is ~45% Hispanic), hurricane-season "store closed" banners. Other cities: NY sales tax on clothing over $110, California privacy law (CCPA) for apps, Illinois biometric law (BIPA) for apps using face/fingerprint |
| **Local proof** | Real local clients first. Until then, the matching concept brand clearly labeled "Example project" |
| Local industries row | Links to our industry pages, framed for the city ("Houston restaurants paying 30% to delivery apps") |
| Working together remotely | Honest: "We're a remote team. Calls 9–5 Central, weekly demos on video, one in-person visit for projects over $X" (only if the owner agrees to travel) |
| City FAQ | "Do you have an office in Houston?" (honest no) · "Do you work Central Time?" · "Can you set up Texas sales tax?" · "Have you worked with Houston businesses?" |
| Shared blocks (≤50%) | Pricing tiers, process, general FAQ, CTA, the same as the service page |

**Rules:**
- No fake addresses, no "our Houston office", no virtual-office Google Business Profiles. Those get suspended and can trigger a manual action.
- Title: `Shopify Agency in Houston · Stores from $4,900 · [Brand]`. Use **"Top-rated"** or **"Best"** only once we have the reviews to back it (e.g. "Top-rated on Clutch" with 15+ reviews). Lumina says "Best" because it has 57 five-star reviews.
- Each city page is written by a person who researched that city. AI drafting is fine; generic city filler isn't.
- Canonical to itself; linked from the city hub, the service page ("We also work with businesses in New York, Houston, Miami…") and `/locations`.
- Schema: `Service` with `areaServed: {"@type":"City","name":"Houston"}`. **Not** `LocalBusiness` (we have no premises there).

## 7. The authority stack (what actually made Lumina rank)

City pages alone won't rank in US metros. These carry them:

| Lumina has | Our equivalent | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify Partner directory, 57 reviews, 5.0 | Join Shopify Partners now; every Shopify client leaves a directory review. Set "supported locations" to United States. | **P1** |
| Clutch awards per country/category | Clutch profile + 15 verified reviews → eligible for Clutch "Top [service] Company" awards. Clutch ranks by review volume and recency. | **P1** |
| DesignRush, Sortlist profiles | DesignRush, Sortlist, GoodFirms, UpCity profiles with US service area | P1 |
| Listed in "top Shopify agencies in Dubai" roundups | Pitch inclusion in "top Shopify agencies in [city/USA]" and "best app developers in [city]" roundups, once we have reviews | P2 |
| Real UAE clients | Real US clients per city, each turned into a case study tagged with the city | P1 (ongoing) |
| Video testimonials | 60-second client video per case study | P2 |

Where Clutch shows **location**: it lists agencies under the cities where they have offices. A remote studio can usually list only its real base. So Clutch helps our **category** rankings (Shopify, app development) and brand trust, not city lists, unless we open a real US presence.

## 8. Decision for the owner: where is "home"?

Lumina ranks hardest in its home city. Which city we can honestly call home changes the plan:

- **If the team or a partner is actually in a US city** (or the owner will have one there), that city becomes our "Dubai": a real address, a Google Business Profile, Clutch location, local meetups. Rank there first, like Lumina.
- **If we're fully remote outside the US**, layers 1–2 above are the route. Expect city pages to take longer (6–12 months) than Lumina's did, and lean harder on reviews.
- **For the UAE/Arabic launch later**, Lumina's model applies almost directly: Dubai-focused service pages, AED prices, VAT, Tabby/Tamara, Arabic. That's where we should copy their structure and add the local depth their pages lack.

## 9. Measurement for location pages

- Search Console, filtered by page `/locations/`: impressions and average position per city × service.
- Kill or merge any city page with zero impressions after 4 months. Fewer strong pages beat many weak ones.
- Track leads by city (form field "Where's your business?") to decide wave 2.
