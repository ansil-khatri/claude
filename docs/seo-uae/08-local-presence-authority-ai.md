# 08 · Local presence, authority and AI search (UAE)

Lumina ranks for "best Shopify agency in Dubai" mostly because of **where it's based and what others say about it**: a Dubai base, 57 five-star reviews in Shopify's partner directory, Clutch UAE awards, DesignRush/Sortlist profiles and real UAE clients. This file is how we build the same, faster.

## 1. Local presence: the foundation

| Step | Cost (approx., 2026) | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Free-zone trade licence with IT/software activity** (IFZA ~AED 12,900, Meydan ~AED 12,500, Dubai Silicon Oasis ~AED 11,900; Ajman ~AED 4,888 is cheapest but not Dubai) | AED 5k–18k/yr incl. visa options | Legal UAE business, invoices in AED, local bank account, "Licensed in Dubai" trust line, Clutch/DesignRush UAE location, `.ae` domain |
| **+971 number + WhatsApp Business** | Low | Local trust, WhatsApp CTA |
| **Address** (flexi-desk ~AED 5,000/yr, or a real co-working desk) | AED 5k+/yr | Address on site and directories |
| **Google Business Profile** | Free | The Google Maps pack for "web design Dubai", "app developer near me" (a big extra source of leads) |

**Google Business Profile caution:** Google requires a real location that's **staffed during business hours** where customers can meet you, with permanent signage. Many flexi-desk/virtual addresses get profiles suspended. Options:
1. A real staffed co-working desk with signage → full profile with address.
2. A **service-area business** profile (address hidden, service area = Dubai/UAE) only if we actually visit clients.
3. No profile until we have a qualifying office. Organic rankings and directories still work without it.

Note for free-zone companies: some free-zone licences restrict trading directly with mainland UAE clients. Confirm with the free zone that IT services to mainland businesses are allowed (usually fine for services, but check the activity).

## 6. Proof must be real (before launch) ⚠️

The current mockups use US concept brands with invented results (Denver, Austin, "+212% orders", client quotes, star ratings). For the UAE site:
1. **Localize the concept brands** for design (Dubai areas, AED, WhatsApp, Talabat), **and**
2. On the live site, label every invented result "Example project · illustrative numbers", or replace it with a real client result with written permission.

Fake testimonials and invented results can break UAE consumer protection rules, break Google's review and spam policies, and sink trust on Clutch and in AI answers. Two real Dubai case studies beat ten polished fakes. Start collecting now: every project gets a before/after, 3 numbers, a quote and permission.
