## 1. Where we sit in the UAE market

| Who | Small business website | Business app |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer / very cheap shops | AED 1,500–5,000 | AED 8,500–45,000 |
| Boutique Dubai agency | AED 8,000–18,000 | AED 45,000–90,000 |
| Large Dubai agency | AED 18,000–40,000+ | AED 70,000–120,000+ |
| **Us: studio quality, fixed price, Arabic + English** | **AED 7,900–19,900** | **AED 35,000–105,000** |

**Positioning line:** *"Big-agency quality at boutique prices. Fixed in AED before we start, Arabic and English included, and you own everything."* We sit at the low end of boutique: cheaper than the big Dubai agencies, clearly better than freelancers (team, process, contract, code ownership).

Note on USD conversion: AED is pegged at 3.6725 per USD. The US tiers converted directly would sit at the top of the UAE range for websites, so website and Shopify tiers are set lower here; app and software tiers stay close to the US ones because UAE app prices are similar or higher.

## 4. Payment terms

- Under AED 20,000: 50% to start, 50% at launch.
- Over AED 20,000: 30% to start, 40% at design approval / mid-build demo, 30% at launch.
- Monthly plans billed monthly, 30 days' notice to cancel. **No 6-month lock-ins** (most Dubai SEO agencies require them, so "no contract" is a selling point).
- Pay by bank transfer, card or payment link. Tax invoices issued (and e-invoices once the mandate applies to us).
- The client owns code, designs and accounts (Shopify, Apple/Google developer accounts, domains) from the first payment.

## 5. Owner decisions

1. **Shopify from AED 9,900 vs Lumina's ~$1,500 (≈ AED 5,500) entry.** Lumina's entry is for small jobs; our AED 9,900 is a full store. Keep, but consider an "AED 4,900 Shopify setup" add-on for owners who already have a theme.
2. **Website from AED 7,900** is within the boutique range and clearly above the AED 1,500 shops. Keep.
3. **Apps from AED 35,000 / 49,000** are at the bottom of boutique agencies (AED 45–90k). Keep: it's our strongest price advantage.
4. **Show prices at all?** Most Dubai agencies hide them. Showing AED prices is our biggest differentiator and wins "cost" searches. Recommended.
