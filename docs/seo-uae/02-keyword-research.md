# 02 · Keyword research: Dubai + UAE

Every keyword has one owner page (`→`), so pages never compete with each other.

## How to read this

- **UAE volumes are about 1/20–1/40 of US volumes.** Bands are adjusted for the UAE:

| Band | Monthly UAE searches (est.) | Meaning here |
|---|---|---|
| **H** | 500+ | Head terms. Mostly owned by old local agencies. Earn later. |
| **M** | 100–500 | Main battlefield. |
| **L** | 20–100 | **The money zone:** low volume, buyer intent, low competition. One client from one of these keywords can be worth AED 20,000+. |
| **E** | <20 or new | Emerging (e-invoicing, Tabby integration, vibe rescue). Cheap to win now. |

- **Estimated bands, not measured numbers.** Validate each primary keyword in Google Keyword Planner with location = United Arab Emirates and language = English or Arabic before writing.
- **KD:** Easy / Med / Hard, judged from who ranks today (see `01` §2).
- **Intent:** 💰 hire now · 🧮 cost · ⚖️ comparing · 📚 learning.
- **Priority:** P1 launch · P2 months 2–4 · P3 months 5–12.
- **Language:** EN = English page, AR = Arabic page (`/ar/...`). Arabic keywords are written as UAE owners type them. **Have a native Arabic writer check every Arabic keyword and its common variants** (with and without "ال", "في دبي" vs "دبي", الامارات vs الإمارات).

## The UAE searcher

1. **The owner who isn't technical** (restaurants, salons, garages, AC companies, laundries, shops). They search their situation and the platform they're stuck with: "talabat commission", "whatsapp order system for restaurant", "fresha alternative", "e-invoicing uae what to do". Many add **"dubai"** or **"uae"** to everything.
2. **The informed buyer** (ecommerce managers, founders, marketing heads): "shopify agency dubai", "tabby integration shopify", "mvp development dubai".
3. **The agency** (Dubai has hundreds of marketing agencies that outsource dev): "white label web development dubai".
4. **The Arabic-first owner** (Emiratis, Saudi buyers, many Arab expats): "تصميم متجر الكتروني", "برمجة تطبيقات".

## N · Avoid

- "web design dubai", "seo company dubai" as **primary** targets at launch (hundreds of entrenched agencies; we earn them in year 2).
- "cheap website dubai", "website AED 999" (wrong buyer).
- Jobs, internships, "freelance developer dubai" (sellers).
- Gulf countries beyond the UAE until the UAE ranks (Saudi = phase 2, Arabic).

## O · Validation checklist (per page)

1. Keyword Planner, location UAE, language EN and AR: confirm band and CPC. **High CPC + low volume = the money keyword.**
2. Search from the UAE (VPN or Search Console data later): who ranks, which format, "People also ask" → copy into the FAQ.
3. Read 3 threads on Reddit (r/dubai, r/UAE, r/DubaiJobs is noise; r/smallbusiness), Facebook groups (Dubai entrepreneurs, Dubai restaurant owners) and Quora for real phrasing.
4. Arabic: native writer confirms phrasing and variants.
