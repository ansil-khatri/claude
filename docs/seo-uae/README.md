## The strategy in 8 lines

1. **Dubai + UAE is the home market**, the way Lumina's is. Every service page is titled "… in Dubai & UAE". Abu Dhabi gets its own pages later, Sharjah after that.
2. **Lead with Shopify, WhatsApp and e-invoicing.** Shopify is our best head term (Lumina shows thin pages win). WhatsApp is how UAE customers talk to businesses (85% prefer it). E-invoicing becomes mandatory for SMEs on **1 July 2027**, so search demand is rising and few agencies explain it well.
3. **Don't fight "web design Dubai" or "SEO company Dubai" first.** Hundreds of entrenched agencies hold them. Win industry, problem and cost searches first; head terms come in year 2.
4. **Target non-technical owners in their own words:** "talabat commission", "whatsapp ordering for restaurant", "fresha alternative", "tabby integration", "website cost dubai". These are the low-volume, high-value keywords.
5. **Bilingual:** English first, Arabic for the top ~10 pages by month 3, written by a native writer. Arabic SEO is a gap most Dubai agencies don't fill well.
6. **Show AED prices.** Most Dubai agencies hide them. Ours sit at the low end of boutique agencies, with Arabic included and no 6-month lock-in.
7. **Get a real local base:** a free-zone licence, +971 number and an address make us "based in Dubai" honestly, unlock Clutch UAE and possibly Google Maps. Then build reviews (Shopify directory, Clutch) fast. That's Lumina's real advantage.
8. **Real proof before launch.** US concept brands and invented results must be localized for the mockups and labeled "Example" on the live site, or replaced with real UAE clients.

## What to measure (monthly)

- Leads from organic and AI: `whatsapp_click`, `booking_completed`, by page and language.
- Search Console: clicks and positions per money page (EN and AR separately).
- P1 keywords in the top 10.
- Shopify directory and Clutch review counts; referring domains; brand searches.
- AI prompt log (`08` §5).
- Core Web Vitals (mobile).

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Brand name and domain** (`.com`; also buy the `.ae` once licensed and redirect it).
2. **Local base:** which free zone, and whether we can have a staffed desk (decides Google Maps eligibility).
3. **AED prices** in `06` (they replace the USD homepage prices for the UAE site).
4. **Localize the design?** The approved mockups are US-based (Denver/Austin, USD, iMessage, DoorDash). For the UAE launch they need UAE versions of the concept brands (e.g. Ember Kitchen in JLT, FreshFold in Dubai Marina, Apex Auto in Al Quoz, an AC company instead of Volt & Wire/Peak Plumbing). This is design work on all 18 pages.
5. **Arabic writer:** who writes and reviews Arabic pages?
6. **Service names:** OK to rename "WordPress development" → "Website design" and "AI integration" → "WhatsApp & AI" for UAE search language?
7. **Real clients:** who are the first 5 UAE clients to ask for reviews and case studies?
