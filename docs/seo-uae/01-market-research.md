# 01 · UAE market research

Written 2026-10-09. These are the facts the UAE plan is built on. Sources are listed at the bottom. Many are agency or vendor blogs, so figures are used as ranges, not exact numbers.

## 1. The market in one page

| Fact | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| ~10–11 million people, ~90% expats; Dubai is the regional business hub | Business owners come from everywhere. Many run their business in English, but Emirati owners and Saudi buyers search in Arabic. |
| Google handles the vast majority of searches; most traffic is mobile | Same SEO playbook as the US, but mobile speed matters even more. |
| **English leads for commercial, tech and B2B searches**; Arabic searches are more question-shaped; one agency estimate says ~60% of all UAE searches are in Arabic (unsourced) | Launch in English, add Arabic for the top pages within 3 months. Arabic pages need a native writer: Arabic SEO commands a premium in Dubai because few do it well. That's an opening. |
| **85% of UAE residents prefer to contact businesses on WhatsApp**; 65% used WhatsApp for a product or service inquiry last year vs 55% call centre and 48% email (Zbooni/YouGov) | **WhatsApp is the UAE's main business channel.** Our AI, automation, ordering and booking offers lead with WhatsApp. "Chat on WhatsApp" becomes a primary CTA next to "Book a call". |
| Shopify ~42% of UAE stores, WooCommerce ~36% (AfterShip estimate); Salla is strong in Saudi/UAE with Tabby/Tamara built in | Shopify and WooCommerce are our ecommerce focus; Salla is the competitor to address in comparisons ("Shopify vs Salla"). |
| Payments: Stripe works in the UAE (~2.9% + AED 1, +1% international cards), plus Telr, Tap, PayTabs, Amazon Payment Services, Network International (N-Genius), Checkout.com, Ziina, Mamo; BNPL via **Tabby** and **Tamara** is expected by shoppers | Payment integration is a strong UAE service: "Tabby integration", "payment gateway integration Dubai", gateway comparisons. |
| Food delivery: Talabat (dominant), Deliveroo (now owned by DoorDash), Careem, Noon Food; reported commissions ~15–30% per order; a Sept 2025 UAE circular pushed platforms on pricing transparency | Restaurant own-ordering pitch works exactly as in the US: "keep the 30%". |
| **UAE e-invoicing becomes mandatory:** pilot from 1 July 2026; businesses with revenue ≥ AED 50m go live 1 Jan 2027; **businesses under AED 50m go live 1 July 2027** (ASP appointed by 31 Mar 2027); government 1 Oct 2027. B2C not yet covered. | **The biggest timely opportunity.** Every SME selling B2B must connect its invoicing/ERP to an Accredited Service Provider. Huge search interest in 2026–27, and it fits our Automation & ERP service. (We integrate, we're not an ASP.) |
| VAT 5%; corporate tax 9% (since 2023) | Prices shown "+5% VAT" once VAT-registered. Bookkeeping and tax-ready automation (Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Odoo, Tally) is a strong sub-topic. |
| Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) + DIFC/ADGM data rules | Content angle for apps and websites: consent, data storage. Trust signal. |

## 2. Competition: who ranks today

| Query type | Who's on page 1 | Difficulty for a new site |
|---|---|---|
| "web design company Dubai" / "website design Dubai" | Dozens of local agencies with exact-match domains (webdesignindubai.com, browser.ae, redspider.ae, grafdom, webcastle) plus Clutch/DesignRush lists | **Very hard.** Don't lead with this. Win the long tail and industries first. |
| "SEO company Dubai" | Clutch UAE lists, edirect, beontop, many "No.1 SEO company" pages | **Very hard.** Skip as a head term. |
| "app development company Dubai" | Big international agencies with `/ae/` sections (Cubix, Tekrevol, Apptunix, Netguru guides) + Clutch | Hard. **Proves a non-local agency can rank in Dubai with a UAE section.** Target the SME niches they ignore (restaurant app, booking app, delivery app). |
| "Shopify agency Dubai" | Lumina (Dubai-based, 57 Shopify directory reviews), CartCoders and Magenest roundups, DesignRush UAE | Medium. Thin pages win here. **Our best head-term target.** |
| Cost queries ("website cost Dubai", "app development cost Dubai") | Agency cost guides (Element8, Digital Gravity, Redberries, Cubix) | Medium. Most are vague; ours will have real AED tiers. |
| E-invoicing, Tabby integration, WhatsApp chatbots, Talabat alternatives, Arabic queries | Vendor blogs, consultancies, few agencies | **Easy to medium. Our fastest wins.** |

## 4. UAE-specific industries

The six existing industry pages all fit the UAE, with local adjustments:

| Industry | UAE reality | Local competitors/platforms to address |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurants & cloud kitchens | Huge delivery culture, many cloud kitchens, 15–30% commissions | Talabat, Deliveroo, Careem, Noon Food; POS: Foodics, Toast, Square, Revel |
| Salons & spas (incl. home salon services) | Very large market, ladies' salons, at-home services | Fresha (Dubai-born), Vagaro, Booksy |
| Laundry & dry cleaning | Pickup/delivery is the norm | Washmen, Laundry apps; POS: CleanCloud |
| Auto repair & car care | Garages in Al Quoz and Ras Al Khor, at-home car service, high car ownership | CarSwitch, garage aggregators |
| Home services | **AC maintenance and repair** (year-round), cleaning, maids, handyman, pest control | Justlife, ServiceMarket, Urban Company |
| Phone repair & electronics | Deira, Bur Dubai, Al Karama; trade-ins | Marketplaces, mall kiosks |

**New UAE industries to add later (high demand, high budgets):** real estate brokerages (huge in Dubai), clinics and medical centres (DHA/DOH licensing), car rental, perfume/oud and abaya/fashion brands (Shopify!), gyms and studios, cleaning and maid companies.

## 5. What this changes versus the US plan

1. Prices in **AED**, "+5% VAT".
2. **WhatsApp everywhere**: CTA, AI assistants, ordering, notifications, mockups (WhatsApp instead of iMessage).
3. **E-invoicing 2027** becomes a lead-generation pillar.
4. **Bilingual** site (English first, Arabic for top pages).
5. **Local presence** decides how far we can go (Maps, Clutch UAE location, trust). See `08`.
6. **Mockups and concept brands are US-based** today (Denver, Austin, Phoenix, USD, iMessage, DoorDash). For a UAE launch they need UAE versions: Dubai neighbourhoods, AED, WhatsApp, Talabat, Tabby. **Owner decision** (see README).
