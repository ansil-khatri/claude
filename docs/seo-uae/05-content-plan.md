# 05 · Content plan: guides, tools and the e-invoicing opportunity

## 1. How we write for UAE owners

1. **Start from a real UAE moment.** "It's Friday 9 pm in JLT, your Talabat tablet is beeping and three customers are asking on WhatsApp if you deliver to Marina." Then the problem, then the answer.
2. **Answer in the first 2–3 sentences** (a number in AED, a date, a yes/no). That's what AI Overviews and ChatGPT quote.
3. **Use local names and numbers:** AED, VAT, Talabat, Tabby, Foodics, Zoho, free zones, Dubai areas. A US article with "Dubai" pasted in reads as spam, to people and to Google.
4. **Be honest, including "you don't need us":** "Under 30 orders a day? A WhatsApp Business catalogue and a payment link are enough. Here's how."
5. **Plain English**, short sentences. Many readers have English as a second language. Explain every tech word once.
6. **Arabic versions are rewritten, not translated**, by a native writer, with Arabic examples and phrasing.
7. **A real named author and reviewer** with photo and LinkedIn. AI drafts are fine as a start; a human adds real experience.
8. **Dates and laws are checked.** E-invoicing and VAT articles show "Last checked against Ministry of Finance guidance on [date]" and are reviewed monthly until July 2027.
9. Lengths: cost pillars 2,500–3,500 words; problem posts 1,200–1,800; comparisons 1,800–2,500.
10. Soft CTA twice: "Want us to look at yours? WhatsApp us or book a 30-minute call."

## 3. The e-invoicing opportunity (start now)

Every UAE business selling B2B must issue e-invoices through an Accredited Service Provider (ASP): businesses with revenue ≥ AED 50m from **1 January 2027**, everyone else from **1 July 2027** (ASP appointed by 31 March 2027). Searches will climb all through 2026–27, most SMEs don't know what to do, and their accounting tools (Zoho Books, Odoo, QuickBooks, Tally, custom systems) need connecting. That connection work is our Automation & ERP service.
