Written 2026-10-10 for the Parrot Noir Next.js build. ## Read this first: what this plan can and cannot promise
1. **Search volumes here are estimates.** I have no Ahrefs, Semrush or Search Console access. Bands (XS under 30, S 30 to 100, M 100 to 500, L 500 plus per month in the UAE) come from SERP depth, how many agencies target the phrase, autocomplete-style variants and benchmark pricing pages. Validate the top 40 in Google Keyword Planner (free with an Ads account) and, after launch, Search Console. Re-rank by real impressions in month 2.
2. **Reddit could not be queried** from my tools (blocked). Quora and the open web could, and they are used below. The Reddit step is a 90-minute manual task for you: see the method at the end of `01`.
3. **Nobody can guarantee "best agency in the UAE" or in the USA.** Google and AI assistants infer "best" from reviews, directory listings, mentions and links on other sites, not from our own claims. The site makes us eligible; `06` is the plan to earn the rest. Realistic timing: long-tail and cost pages move in 6 to 12 weeks; head terms like "shopify agency in dubai" take 6 to 12 months and need reviews and links.
4. **Market scope.** You wrote both "UAE" and "USA". The site, prices and proof are built for Dubai and the UAE, so the UAE is the launch market. The US is a second phase on `/us/` with its own prices, pages and proof (`06`). Do not run both at once: a thin US section would dilute the UAE site.

## The strategy in eight lines
1. **Think like a client, not a developer.** Owners search their situation ("website for my business", "reply to WhatsApp automatically", "stop using Excel"), not stacks. Every service gets a plain-language problem page or guide.
2. **Own the money questions:** cost, "which is better", "how long", "who to hire". Publish real AED figures; they convert and AI answers quote them.
3. **One URL per intent.** 14 exact-match money pages exist; add 8 gap pages, 10 industry pages, 4 tools, 1 free audit.
4. **Industry pages are the conversion engine.** "Shopify for perfume brands", "website for a clinic". Lower volume, much higher intent, little competition.
5. **Every page answers first** (40 to 60 words), then shows proof (mockup, price, process), then asks one clear next step.
6. **Hub and spoke linking** with fixed rules (`02`), so authority flows to the money pages.
7. **Proof is the bottleneck.** The six builds are concepts. The first real client, review and Clutch/Shopify listing will lift conversion more than any page. Plan for it.
8. **Measure to money:** WhatsApp click, form, booked call, quote requested, by landing page.
