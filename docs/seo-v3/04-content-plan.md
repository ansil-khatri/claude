# 04 · Content plan: 36 posts, 4 waves

15 posts exist (10 written for v3, 5 reused). 21 are new. Every post answers one question a real owner asks, feeds one money page, and ends with the action that fits the reader's stage.

## Writing brief (every post)
1. **Title** = the question or the cost phrase, plus the year when price-sensitive. 2. **Answer box** (40 to 60 words, a real number). 3. **Opening** names the situation, not the technology. 4. **Body**: H2 per sub-question, a table where numbers exist, one mockup or screenshot, one "what we would do" paragraph with honest trade-offs. 5. **FAQ** 4 to 6, from PAA/Quora wording. 6. **Links** per `02` rule 3. 7. **CTA** matched to stage. 8. 1,200 to 1,800 words unless a calculator/checklist. 9. Sources named inline for any statistic; our own numbers are marked as ours. 10. Date + "Updated" visible.
