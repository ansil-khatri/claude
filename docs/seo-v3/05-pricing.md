# 05 · Pricing

## Principles
Publish a starting price for every service (it converts and AI assistants quote it), show typical totals, say what is excluded, and quote fixed after a call. Never price below the cost of senior time to win a lead; compete on clarity and fit.

## What the market says (sources are agency blogs: ranges, not facts)
- UAE small-business website: about AED 3,500 to 15,000; corporate AED 12,000 to 55,000; e-commerce AED 8,000 to 150,000 plus ([Cubix](https://www.cubix.co/ae/blog/web-development-cost-in-dubai/), [Element8](https://www.element8.ae/blogs/website-development-cost-dubai), [Digital Gravity](https://www.digitalgravity.ae/blog/how-much-does-shopify-development-cost-in-dubai/)). A Shopify fashion store with 100+ products is cited at AED 42,000.
- Freelancers AED 75 to 250 per hour; agency retainers AED 6,000 to 18,000 per month ([Cubix e-commerce](https://www.cubix.co/ae/blog/ecommerce-development-cost-in-dubai/)). Maintenance is usually 10 to 15% of build cost per year.
- Local SEO retainers AED 1,500 to 8,000 per month for SMEs ([Red Spider](https://www.redspider.ae/blog/why-website-not-ranking-google-dubai/)).
- WhatsApp: Meta fees per message plus a provider fee plus build; custom AI chatbots for B2B USD 15,000 to 40,000 ([SleekFlow](https://sleekflow.io/blog/whatsapp-business-price-uae), [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/custom-ai-customer-service-chatbot-cost-dubai-2026)).
- Bilingual RTL sites add roughly 15 to 30% ([Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/multilingual-website-build-cost-dubai-rtl-2026)).
- Shopify Plus platform cost about USD 2,300 per month (3-year) or 2,500 (1-year); US Plus agency builds USD 25,000 to 100,000 plus ([Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/shopify-plus-partner-agency)).

## Quote and payment rules (keep)
Under AED 20,000: 50% start, 50% at launch. Above: 30/40/30. Care plans monthly, 30 days' notice. Change requests priced before work. Accounts, code, domain in the client's name.

## Where to show prices
`/pricing` (full table), the first screen of every money page, the answer box, schema `Offer`, `llms.txt`. Never hide a price behind a form: that is what competitors do and why a clear table wins clicks.
