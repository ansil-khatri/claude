How it works

Here's exactly what happens
after you book a call.

No black box. You know what happens each week, what you need to do, and when it goes live.

01
Day 1 · 30 minutes

We talk about your business.

Not tech. Your customers, your bottlenecks, what you want to sell or save. We'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.

You: join a video call
02
Within 48 hours

You get a plan and a fixed price.

A written proposal: what we'll build, every feature, the timeline week by week, and one fixed price. No hourly surprises.

You: read it, ask questions
03
Week 1

We agree and kick off.

A simple agreement: you own the code, scope and price are fixed. You get a dedicated project manager and a shared channel.

You: sign and pay the first milestone
04
Weeks 2–4

We design every screen in Figma.

Real designs, not wireframes: every page of the website and every screen of the app, in your brand. Click through it on your own phone.

You: leave comments right on the designs
05
End of week 4

You approve. Nothing gets built before that.

We revise until it's right. Only when you say "approved" does development start, so what you see is exactly what you get.

You: click "Approve"
06
Weeks 5–9

We build the website and the apps.

Web, iOS and Android in parallel. From day one you get a live staging link, and every Friday a short demo video of what's new.

You: watch a 5-minute demo each week
07
Weeks 10–11

We test everything. Then test again.

Real iPhones, Android phones, tablets and browsers. Payments, emails, edge cases, speed and security. You and your staff test too, with real orders.

You: try it with your team
08
Week 12

Go live.

We publish to the App Store and Google Play, connect your domain, set up analytics and Google Search, and train your team.

You: announce it to your customers
09
After launch

We stay with you.

Hosting, updates, fixes and new features on a simple monthly plan, plus a monthly report showing what your new system did for the business.

You: grow
