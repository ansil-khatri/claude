Landing page FAQ

Questions before you build a page.

Your homepage has to talk to everyone, so it has a menu, several services and lots of ways to wander off. A landing page talks to one person about one thing: the exact thing they just searched for. That focus is usually what takes a page from 1–2% of visitors becoming leads to 6–10%.

We write them. We start from your ads, your reviews and a short call about what customers ask you most, then send you the text to approve before anything is designed. You know your business; we know what makes people tap the button.

We focus on the page, the tracking and the lead follow-up. If you already have someone running your Google or Meta ads, we work alongside them. If you don't, we can introduce you to ad managers we trust.

Yes. Headlines, prices, offers and photos can be edited in a simple editor, with no code. Bigger changes, like a new section or a new page, are quick for us to add.

Usually in the first week after launch, as soon as the ads point at the new page. We compare the first 30 days with the 30 days before, using the same ad budget, so you can see the difference in real numbers.
